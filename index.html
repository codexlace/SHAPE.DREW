The ritual

One card. One creature. One useful lesson.

  1. Seed: pick the character ingredients.
  2. Shape: get the construction recipe.
  3. Readability: test the silhouette.
  4. Practice: draw one tiny drill.
  5. Save: keep the card in your Toybox.
The face is the steering wheel. Place it low when you want cute, shy, or plushy.
