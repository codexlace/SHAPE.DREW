Beginner rescue rules

One weird thingDo not add twelve symbols. Give the character one unmistakable signature.
Big firstSilhouette, pose, expression. Tiny decorations come after.
Readable faceTwo eyes and a mouth can carry more feeling than a pile of detail.
Trace allowedTracing your own blueprint is practice. It trains hand confidence.
